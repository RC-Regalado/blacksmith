## Status

automatically-accepted

Implemented together with M5.2.8, M5.2.10, M5.2.11 and M5.2.12 (same change set, same files). ## Scope delivered

`ai_assistant/application/tool_loop.py`: `fingerprint(tool_name, arguments) -> str` (order-independent, via `json.dumps(..., sort_keys=True, default=str)`) and `DuplicateCallGuard.is_duplicate(history, candidate) -> bool`, which flags a candidate fingerprint that appears anywhere in the turn's `ToolCallAttempt` history regardless of whether the prior attempt succeeded or failed — a byte-for-byte repeat cannot add new evidence, so it is rejected without re-executing. Remediates the "identical failing call retried verbatim" half of FINDING-004.

Dedicated unit tests in `tests/test_tool_loop.py`: `test_fingerprint_is_stable_regardless_of_argument_order`, `test_fingerprint_differs_for_different_arguments`, `test_duplicate_guard_flags_exact_repeat_of_a_success`, `test_duplicate_guard_flags_exact_repeat_of_a_failure`, `test_duplicate_guard_allows_a_changed_strategy`. Integration proof in `tests/test_agent_runtime.py::test_runtime_rejects_duplicate_tool_call_without_re_executing` (asserts the coordinator is invoked exactly once despite two identical requests).
