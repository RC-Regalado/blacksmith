## Summary

M5.9 adds local SQLite FTS5 lexical search over derived knowledge chunks.

Implemented:

- `knowledge_chunks_fts` virtual table.
- FTS synchronization during `save_chunks()`, `delete_document()` and `clear()`.
- `KnowledgeQuery.metadata_filters`.
- `KnowledgeStore.lexical_search()`.
- Freshness, source type and metadata filtering.
- `RetrievalCandidate` results with document/chunk provenance.

Not implemented in this milestone:

- Manual knowledge CLI.
- Embeddings.
- Hybrid retrieval or ranking.
- Context compilation or planner/synthesis integration.
