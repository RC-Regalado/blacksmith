## Status

implemented-awaiting-human-review

## Summary

M5.15 adds bounded context compilation from ranked retrieval candidates.

Implemented:

- `ContextCompiler`.
- Compilation from `RankedCandidate` to `CompiledContext`.
- Chunk text loading from `KnowledgeStore`.
- Provenance verification with `content_hash` and `token_count`.
- Freshness enforcement.
- ContextBudget enforcement by omission of over-budget candidates.

Not implemented in this milestone:

- Planner integration.
- Synthesis integration.
- Runtime context injection.
- Retrieval metrics.
