# Milestone Report — M5.6 Hashing and Invalidation

## Status

implemented-awaiting-human-review

## Summary

M5.6 adds minimal content hashing and freshness invalidation primitives for derived knowledge.

Implemented:

- `hash_text()` and `hash_bytes()` SHA-256 helpers.
- `KnowledgeStore.document_is_current()`.
- `KnowledgeStore.mark_document_stale()`.
- `KnowledgeStore.mark_missing_documents_stale()`.
- SQLite invalidation for documents and chunks.

Not implemented in this milestone:

- Source adapters.
- Normalization or chunk generation.
- FTS5, embeddings, retrieval, ranking or context compilation.
