## Scope delivered

`ai_assistant/application/tool_loop.py`: `ProgressGuard(max_consecutive_failures=2).is_stalled(history) -> bool`, which stops the loop once the most recent N attempts (N = `max_consecutive_failures`) are all failures (`DENIED`/`ERROR`/`TIMEOUT`) with zero success in between — deliberately narrower than the audit's suggested "reject an unrelated tool after failure if no progress rationale is available," which would require non-deterministic semantic judgment forbidden by the binding constraints. This scope decision is intentional and documented in code: `ProgressGuard` genuinely adds value beyond `DuplicateCallGuard` (it also catches a *sequence* of distinct-but-unproductive failing calls, not just exact repeats) while staying fully deterministic and testable. Partially remediates the "changed-strategy-but-still-failing" half of FINDING-004.

Dedicated unit tests in `tests/test_tool_loop.py`: `test_progress_guard_allows_a_single_failure`, `test_progress_guard_stops_after_repeated_failures_with_no_success`, `test_progress_guard_allows_continuation_after_a_success`. Integration proof in `tests/test_agent_runtime.py::test_runtime_stops_via_progress_guard_after_repeated_failures` (asserts the coordinator is invoked exactly twice, then the loop halts and synthesizes rather than issuing a third failing call).
