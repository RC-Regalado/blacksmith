## Implemented

- Added `.github/workflows/ci.yml`.
- Separated jobs:
  - `unit`
  - `integration`
  - `contract`
  - `smoke`
  - `ollama-smoke`
- Ollama smoke installs Ollama, starts the local daemon, pulls `gemma3:1b` and runs only `pytest -m ollama`.

## Acceptance Criteria Evidence
