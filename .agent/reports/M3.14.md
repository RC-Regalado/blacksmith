- Made `AI_ASSISTANT_TOOL_EXECUTOR=unix_socket` the default productive executor.

- Contract tests cover all tools: `tests/test_c_toolserver_contract.py` covers `list_directory`, `read_file`, `file_metadata`, `search_text`, `git_status`, `git_diff`, `run_tests`, `build_project` and `write`.
